FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Ajar Real Estate?

Ajar Real Estate is a property video marketing service in Nepal run by Ajar Gurung. It produces property videos and promotes them with targeted ads so listings reach real buyers and bring in leads, not just likes.

How does the process work?

Four steps: script the story and who the property is for, shoot the property, edit a tight video made for phones, then boost it with strategic posting and targeted ads in the right area.

Do you run ads for property listings?

Yes. Targeted ads run alongside every listing, so the right buyers see it rather than whoever the algorithm happens to pick.

What results have the videos achieved?

A house tour in Naya Gaun, Pokhara passed 320K organic views on TikTok and generated 500+ leads for the owner. An Instagram reel reached almost 50K views through paid ads and generated 150+ leads.
