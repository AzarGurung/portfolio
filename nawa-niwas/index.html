When I moved out of a hostel in Kathmandu, finding a room meant Facebook groups, walking around neighbourhoods and eventually paying a broker just to get started. Three years later it’s still that hard. Not because there aren’t rooms, but because the listings are scattered, out of date, or behind a broker fee.

I built Nawa Niwas so landlords can list in a few minutes from their phone and renters can contact them directly, with no commission on either side. Listings from agencies are marked, so renters know who they’re calling.

It’s free for everyone, and it went live on 20 September 2026. The plan is to earn later from ads by moving, cleaning, plumbing and electrical services, not from renters or landlords.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Nawa Niwas?

Nawa Niwas (नव निवास) is Nepal’s zero commission rental marketplace at nawaniwas.com. Landlords list rooms, flats, houses and commercial space for free, and renters contact them directly. It launched on 20 September 2026.

Who founded Nawa Niwas?

Ajar Gurung, a Kathmandu-based social media specialist and entrepreneur. Ajar built it after finding that renting a room in Kathmandu meant scattered, out of date listings and broker fees.

Does Nawa Niwas charge commission?

No. Nawa Niwas is free for renters and landlords, with no commission on either side. The plan is to earn later from ads by moving, cleaning, plumbing and electrical services.

How do I list a property on Nawa Niwas?

Publish a listing in four steps from your phone at nawaniwas.com. Listings stay up for three months and renew when you update them. If you’d rather not do it yourself, call Nawa Niwas and the team will create the listing under your name.

What can I rent on Nawa Niwas?

Rooms, flats, houses, shutters and offices across Nepal. Every listing shows the rent, photos and location, and agency listings are marked so renters know who they’re calling. As of October 2026 there are 142 live listings across 8 cities.
